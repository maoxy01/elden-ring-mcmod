## Interpretation Limits

- Generated poses and weapon details are illustrative, not approved mesh replacements.
- Waterfowl panel includes a duplicate blade-like shape; implementation should use the held katana and slash trails, not add an extra weapon.
- Static poses cannot prove combo continuity, timing, hitboxes, two-hand attachment, or animation interpolation.
- Effect brightness, debris, and particle count must be bounded and tuned in game; glowing concept lighting is not a renderer specification.
- Colossal-sword overhead panel depicts the swing direction, not a validated contact pose.
- Preserve existing player skins and connected macro-pixel weapon models. No executions; aerial attacks may be used by the appropriate skills.
